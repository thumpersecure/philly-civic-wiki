Welcome to the Philadelphia Civic Wiki, a free, plain-English reference to how Philadelphia is governed: who holds power, what every city department does, how unions and political organizations fit in, and how to get something done when you need the city to act. Philadelphia is both a city and a county, so its government is more tangled than most. This wiki untangles it.

New here? Start with How Philadelphia Government Is Organized, then browse the Department Directory for a one-line answer on every body. Prefer one long document? See the Full Report.
