How the city-county government is built: Mayor, Council, row offices, charter, and the state and federal layers above it.

  • City Commissioners — The three elected officials who run voter registration and elections in Philadelphia as the county board of elections, and how residents work with them.
  • Register of Wills and Clerk of Orphans' Court — The elected office that probates wills, grants letters of administration, issues marriage licenses and serves as clerk of the Orphans' Court Division in Philadelphia.
  • Civil Service Commission — The Charter-created panel that hears city employee appeals, approves civil service regulations and pay plans, and rules on exemptions and residency waivers.
