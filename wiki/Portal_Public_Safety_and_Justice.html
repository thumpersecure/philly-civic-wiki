Public Safety and Justice

From the Philadelphia Civic Wiki, the free guide to how the city works

Police, fire, prisons, courts, defenders, oversight bodies, and the federal prosecutor.

Articles (13)

  • Philadelphia Police Department — The city's municipal police force: how it is organized and led, how it is funded, how residents interact with it, and its record of reform and controversy.
  • Citizens Police Oversight Commission — Philadelphia's civilian police oversight agency created by a 2020 ballot question: its mandate, its limits after a 2025 arbitration ruling, and how to use it.
  • Police Accountability Systems — How misconduct by Philadelphia police officers is investigated and disciplined: Internal Affairs, the Police Board of Inquiry, the DA, civilian oversight, courts, arbitration and consent decrees.
  • Philadelphia Fire Department — The city's fire and emergency medical service: structure, leadership, staffing, how residents use it, and its history, including the 2022 Fairmount fire.
  • Office of Emergency Management — The city office that plans for and coordinates responses to disasters, severe weather and large events, and how residents can sign up for alerts and prepare.
  • Philadelphia Department of Prisons — The city agency that runs Philadelphia's jails: facilities, leadership, population trends, oversight, and the public record on deaths, escapes and staffing.
  • Office of Violence Prevention — The city office, now part of the Division of Safe Neighborhoods under the Office of Public Safety, that funds and coordinates community-based anti-violence programs.
  • First Judicial District of Pennsylvania — Philadelphia's unified state court system, covering the Court of Common Pleas and Municipal Court: how it is governed, funded and staffed, and how judges are chosen.
  • Court of Common Pleas and Municipal Court — The two trial courts of Philadelphia: which cases each handles, their divisions, how a case moves through them, and where landlord-tenant and small claims cases are heard.
  • Defender Association of Philadelphia — The nonprofit public defender that represents most people in Philadelphia who cannot afford a lawyer in criminal and juvenile cases: history, structure, funding and role.
  • Adult Probation and Parole Department — The First Judicial District unit that supervises adults sentenced to county probation or parole in Philadelphia: structure, caseloads, rules, and reform debates.
  • Medical Examiner's Office — The city office that investigates sudden, violent and suspicious deaths in Philadelphia: legal duties, leadership, caseload, and the public record of its leadership turnover and challenges.
  • U.S. Attorney's Office, Eastern District of Pennsylvania — The federal prosecutor's office covering Philadelphia and eight nearby counties: its role, leadership, priorities and notable public-corruption and civil-rights cases.
